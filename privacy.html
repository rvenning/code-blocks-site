Privacy policy

Code Blocks for Confluence · last updated 15 August 2026

The short version

Code Blocks collects nothing, stores nothing and transmits nothing. The app holds no database and requests no Confluence permissions whatsoever.

What the app has access to

Nothing beyond the macro you place on a page. The app declares no scopes in its manifest, so it cannot read your pages, spaces, attachments or user directory, and it cannot act on your behalf. This is verifiable: the permissions an Atlassian Cloud app holds are shown to administrators before installation.

Where your code is stored

The code you enter is saved by Confluence as part of the page, in your own Atlassian site, exactly like any other page content. It is never copied to any system operated by us, because no such system exists. If you uninstall the app, the content remains in your page.

Data leaving your site

None. The app runs entirely on Atlassian’s Forge platform and declares no external network access, which is a requirement of Atlassian’s Runs on Atlassian standard. It cannot call out to any third party, including us.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no telemetry, no cookies and no tracking of any kind. We do not know who has installed it, which pages it appears on, or what code you put in it.

This website is served as static files by GitHub Pages, which may record standard server access logs. We do not add analytics to it.

Diagnostic logs

The app writes an entry to Atlassian’s own Forge logging only in the specific case where it cannot locate a macro’s settings during an export. That entry records the structure of the data Atlassian passed to the app, not the contents of your code. These logs live in Atlassian’s infrastructure and are subject to Atlassian’s retention.

Personal data

The app stores no personal data. It holds no account identifiers, names or email addresses.

Sub-processors

None. There is no hosting provider, no analytics provider and no third-party service in the path, because the app runs on Atlassian infrastructure and stores nothing.

Billing

Purchases are handled entirely by Atlassian through Marketplace billing. We never see or handle your payment details. Atlassian’s privacy policy governs that relationship.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it. Any change that widened what the app can access would require new permissions, which Atlassian would surface to your administrator for approval before it took effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the address on the support page.
